About Aron

Export-ready Ethiopian coffee and tea, built on origin, quality and partnership.

Aron Coffee & Tea Export PLC is an Ethiopian coffee and tea manufacturer and exporter based in Addis Ababa. The company was established to serve serious international buyers while strengthening long-term partnerships with farmers, distributors and global trade partners.

Our work is grounded in Ethiopian coffee heritage, practical quality assurance and a clear B2B service model. We support buyers seeking reliable supply, clear communication and responsible sourcing from origin.

Quality ExcellenceConsistent product handling and export preparation.
Ethical SourcingFarmer partnership and responsible supply-chain thinking.
